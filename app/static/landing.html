Core Philosophy

The Three Pillars of Epoch

A video tool engineered for truth, musical editing rhythm, and undisputed content ownership.

I.

True.

B-roll must not deceive your audience. When you talk about the 1970 Apollo mission, you shouldn't get a 2024 SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket.

  • Gemini 2.5 Flash reviews candidate video frames
  • Detects era, geography, subjects, weather, and actions
  • Highlights discrepancies before they reach your final render
II.

Cuts.

Good editing has tempo. B-roll cut haphazardly across mid-syllable words sounds sloppy and disorients viewers.

  • Whisper phoneme alignment starts cuts on the keyword
  • Detects speech pauses and breathing gaps for natural ins/outs
  • Leaves host audio completely intact with zero clipping
III.

Yours.

Every piece of footage carries legal consequences. Epoch tracks provenance so you never receive a surprise copyright claim.

  • Automatic CC attribution and commercial clearance verification
  • Generates ready-to-publish end credits and description blocks
  • Supports your own local footage libraries with zero external fees

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use my own footage archives?

Yes, absolutely. Epoch includes a dedicated Libraries tab where you can register any local folder on your SSD or external drive. It automatically segments videos into scene shots and indexes them with embeddings.

How does Gemini verify that a clip is true?

Epoch feeds representative keyframes from the candidate shot alongside your spoken sentence and narrative context to Gemini 2.5 Flash. It checks location markers, era consistency, subjects, and seasonal lighting, warning you if stock footage contradicts your speech.

What happens if a cut starts during a spoken word?

Epoch's conform engine uses phonetic timestamps from Whisper to ensure the cut snaps precisely to the syllable onset or preceding breath pause. Your original microphone audio is conformed losslessly without any cuts or pitch shifts.

Are the stock video clips safe for monetized YouTube videos?

Yes. Clips fetched via the live search integration come under the Pixabay Content License or Pexels License, which permit commercial use without mandatory attribution. Epoch also compiles an exportable credit block so you can paste it directly into your video description.

What video formats can I upload?

Epoch accepts MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, and AVI files up to 1 GB. Outputs are rendered in high-definition H.264 / AAC MP4 ready for immediate upload to YouTube, Vimeo, or social platforms.
